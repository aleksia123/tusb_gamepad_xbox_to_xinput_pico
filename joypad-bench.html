Input monitor

Live gamepad state over CDC. Axes decode exactly as the firmware sends them — 7 bytes, order LX · LY · RX · RY · L2 · R2 · Rz (0–255, sticks centered at 128, Y is HID: 0 up / 255 down). Button bit indices are ground truth from the 32-bit mask; labels are yours to set.
